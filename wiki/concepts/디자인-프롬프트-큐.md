외부 이미지 생성 AI(Midjourney/Stable Diffusion류)에 그대로 복사 붙여넣기할 텍스트 프롬프트 큐. 이 문서 자체는 이미지를 생성하지 않는다 — 사용자가 아래 프롬프트를 외부 AI에 입력해 이미지를 받아온 뒤, 캐릭터는 프레임 좌표만 크롭·리사이즈해 페인터리 그대로 스프라이트화(2026-09-11부터 — 이전엔 Aseprite 도트 트레이싱 방식이었으나 폐기, [[개발-파이프라인-로드맵]] 2단계 참고), 배경/UI는 그대로 또는 재가공해 `raw/assets/`나 `assets/`에 반영하는 흐름을 전제로 한다.

각 항목은 [[에셋-아트-컨셉]]/[[배경-아트-컨셉]]/[[비주얼-디자인-시스템]]에 텍스트로만 정의돼 있고 아직 실제 이미지가 없는 대상 중, 게임 진행상 노출 빈도가 높은 순으로 선정했다. 회차별 상한 5개(카테고리 합산), 이미 이 문서에 등재된 대상은 이후 회차에서 전부 제외한다.

**캐릭터 좌우 방향 규칙:** 전투 캔버스에서 플레이어(목현)는 화면 왼쪽에서 오른쪽(적)을 바라보고, 적 캐릭터는 화면 오른쪽에서 왼쪽(플레이어)을 바라본다. 따라서 적 캐릭터 프롬프트는 프레이밍 지시문에 "character facing left / facing screen-left (opposite of the player-character reference, which faces right)"를 명시해 방향을 반대로 요청한다. 목현(플레이어) 캐릭터 프롬프트는 기존대로 오른쪽을 바라보는 기준을 유지한다.

**도트(픽셀아트) 컨셉 폐기 (2026-09-11):** 아래 혈랑채 프롬프트에 있는 "Pixel art / dot-art... NOT painterly illustration" 문구는 실제 발주 당시 기록이라 원문 그대로 남겨두지만, 이후 신규 캐릭터 프롬프트부터는 도트 스타일을 요청하지 않는다 — 받은 이미지를 프레임만 크롭해 페인터리 그대로 쓰는 것이 현재 파이프라인이므로, 프롬프트도 painterly 스타일을 그대로 요청하면 된다.

## 완료 (이미지 확보·게임 적용됨)

### 캐릭터

#### 혈랑채 두목 (대1 보스)

이미지 확보 완료 — `wiki/raw/assets/혈랑채_보스.png`. [[에셋-아트-컨셉]] §4 참고. 아래는 실제 발주한 프롬프트 원문(도트 요청 문구는 폐기된 과거 방침, 위 안내 참고).

```text
Pixel art / dot-art game sprite sheet (NOT painterly illustration). Subject: 'Blood Wolf Bandit Chief' (혈랑채 두목), the leader of a low-tier bandit gang in a dark wuxia (martial arts fantasy) game, first boss encounter of the game. Role: brute-force gang boss, not a refined martial artist — thuggish and heavy, not lean or elegant.

Appearance: broad-shouldered, pot-bellied, stocky heavyset body type (bulky intimidating brute, NOT a trained swordsman physique). Wears a worn leather vest over bare muscular arms, ragged hemp/burlap trousers, dull muddy brown color palette (base color around #5c4530). Messy unkempt beard, a scar across the forehead, a tattered red headband, crude bone/fang trophy decorations tied to his belt or headband as gang trophies. Wears rough iron pauldron/shoulder armor on one shoulder only (the one piece of metal armor that marks him as the boss, distinguishing him from his mooks). Weapon: a thick, heavy, crude broadsaber (박도) — wide, blunt, brutal blade, NOT a slender elegant sword. Gruff, menacing facial expression.

Deliverable: two separate pixel-art sprite sheets for this same character — (1) an 'idle' animation sheet, a horizontal strip of 6 frames showing a subtle breathing/swaying idle loop with the broadsaber resting on his shoulder; (2) an 'attack' animation sheet, a horizontal strip of 6 frames showing one full swing of the broadsaber from wind-up to follow-through. Each sheet: 6 equal-width cells in a single row, identical canvas size per cell, identical ground baseline across all cells, identical pixel resolution and character scale in every frame. Both sheets must depict the exact same character design, color palette, and pixel resolution — only the pose differs between frames.

Framing/style: front-facing or 3/4 side view, character facing screen-left (opposite of the player-character reference, which faces right — this is an enemy positioned on the right side of the battle screen facing the player), same character proportions and eye-level/baseline as the game's existing protagonist reference sprite (chibi-ish proportions, head:body ratio roughly 1:1.6, large head, simplified stylized anatomy, crisp hard-edged pixel clusters, limited color count per sprite) so the two characters can be scaled consistently in-engine. Solid plain background color or transparent background — no scenery.

Negative prompt: no painterly or cel-shaded illustration style, no smooth gradient shading, no anti-aliasing/blur on edges, no motion blur, no complex or detailed background, no multiple characters, no text or watermark, no camera angle tilt, no inconsistency in character design/proportions/palette between frames.
```

#### 혈랑채 잡몹 3종 (대1 일반 몬스터)

이미지 확보 완료 — `wiki/raw/assets/혈랑채_적.png`. [[에셋-아트-컨셉]] §4 참고. 아래는 실제 발주한 프롬프트 원문(도트 요청 문구는 폐기된 과거 방침, 위 안내 참고).

```text
Pixel art / dot-art game sprite sheets (NOT painterly illustration). Subject: three regular bandit mooks (잡몹) from the 'Blood Wolf Bandit' gang (혈랑채), the common enemies fought across the game's very first stage group (encountered repeatedly, high on-screen frequency). They share the bandit chief's gang identity (same muddy brown leather palette, base color around #5c4530, red accent trophies) but are weaker, downgraded versions — no metal armor pieces at all, only leather.

Three distinct character designs:
1. 'Grunt' (졸개) — thinnest, most scrawny silhouette of the three, wears only a plain leather vest (no other armor), carries a short curved saber (곡도).
2. 'Archer/thrower' (궁수·투척병) — carries a bow or a throwing hand-axe.
3. 'Elite bandit' (정예 산적, a named/rare enemy) — about 1.1x bulkier build than the other two, wears a full leather armor set (still no metal parts, that's reserved for the boss only) to visually read as a step up from the grunts but clearly below the boss.

Deliverable: for EACH of the three designs, produce its own pair of pixel-art sprite sheets — (1) an 'idle' animation sheet, a horizontal strip of 6 frames showing a subtle breathing/weapon-ready idle loop; (2) an 'attack' animation sheet, a horizontal strip of 6 frames showing one full attack motion (grunt: a saber swing; archer/thrower: draw-to-release; elite: a heavier two-handed swing). Each sheet: 6 equal-width cells in a single row, identical canvas size per cell, identical ground baseline across all cells, identical pixel resolution and character scale in every frame. Within one character's idle and attack sheets, the character design, color palette, and pixel resolution must stay identical — only the pose differs between frames.

Framing/style: front-facing or 3/4 side view, character facing screen-left (opposite of the player-character reference, which faces right — these are enemies positioned on the right side of the battle screen facing the player), same proportions and eye-level as the game's protagonist reference sprite (chibi-ish proportions, head:body ratio ~1:1.6, large head, crisp hard-edged pixel clusters, limited color count per sprite) consistent with the protagonist reference's pixel resolution, but using this gang's own muddy-brown palette. Solid plain background, no scenery, no other characters beyond the one specified per sheet.

Negative prompt: no painterly or cel-shaded illustration style, no smooth gradient shading, no anti-aliasing/blur on edges, no motion blur, no complex background, no text or watermark, no extra characters beyond the one specified per sheet, no metal armor on any of these three (armor is reserved for the boss), no inconsistency in character design/proportions/palette between frames.
```

## 대기 중 (이미지 미완성)

### 배경

#### 대2 스테이지 배경 — 흑시장(黑市場)

```text
Painterly 2D game background concept art for a dark wuxia (martial arts fantasy) idle RPG. Location: '흑시장' (Heuksijang, the Black Market) — the second major story chapter's setting, a shady back-alley black market hidden in a merchant district where corrupt brokers illegally extract and trade stolen internal energy (진기) from victims. Story context: the protagonist descends from his mountain sect to the city to earn a living and uncovers this criminal energy-trafficking operation. Wide screen 16:9 composition, no people or characters in the foreground — this is a pure environment/background layer that game character sprites will be composited on top of separately.

Environment: a narrow, cramped back-alley marketplace at night — crooked wooden stalls and market awnings pressed close together, hanging paper lanterns and cloth banners with faded East Asian characters, stacked crates and covered goods implying illicit trade, low eaves and overhanging rooftops creating a claustrophobic, secretive feel. Composition in three depth layers: foreground alley clutter (crates, hanging signs, cobblestone/dirt ground), midground stalls and low buildings, background a hazy skyline of rooftops fading into a dark night sky.

Lighting/color: sickly, unnatural pale teal-green lantern light (hex around #5c7a5a) as the scene's dominant light source — deliberately different and more corrupted/artificial-feeling than the warm gold lantern light used in the game's other 'safe' locations, to convey this place's illicit, unnatural energy. Overall dark wuxia HUD color family: near-black base tones (around #14100e), with bronze/aged-metal accent tones (around #8a6a2c) in structural details, muted warm browns in wood and stone.

Output form: a single wide painterly background illustration, moody and atmospheric, no UI elements.

Negative prompt: no people, no characters, no figures, no text, no watermark, no logo, no daytime lighting, no bright cheerful colors.
```

### UI/이펙트

#### 데미지 숫자 폰트 스타일

```text
Style reference sheet for a stylized damage-number font used in a dark wuxia (martial arts fantasy) idle RPG's combat UI. Purpose: this font style is used for floating damage numbers that pop up over characters every time an attack lands on the game canvas — extremely high-frequency, always-visible UI element, currently just placeholder default system font in the game and needs a proper design.

Deliverable: a style reference sheet showing all digits 0-9 plus a minus sign '-' rendered in the target font/style, laid out in a clean grid, on a solid dark background (near-black, around #100d0a) so the glyph shapes and effects are clearly readable. Also include one example of the full phrase '치명타!' (Korean for 'Critical Hit!') in the same style to show how it reads in a short combat text string, and one example of a plain damage number like '-128' as it would actually appear composited over a dark scene.

Style direction: bold, chunky, easily-readable numerals with a hand-inked brush/calligraphic influence evoking East Asian martial arts aesthetics (subtle brush-stroke texture on the edges, not a generic clean sans-serif), slight outer glow/halo consistent with a dark HUD theme. Two color variants should be shown: a standard-hit variant in warm gold (#ffe27a / #e8bc55 family) and a critical-hit variant in a hotter orange (#ff9800 family) with a slightly stronger glow to read as more impactful. Numerals should feel weighty and punchy, suited to bursts of damage numbers flying up rapidly during fast-paced idle combat.

Negative prompt: no thin/light font weights, no generic default sans-serif look, no Latin-only decorative script unrelated to the brief, no watermark, no background scenery.
```

#### 크리티컬 히트 이펙트

```text
Style reference sheet for a critical-hit visual effect used in a dark wuxia (martial arts fantasy) idle RPG's combat canvas. Purpose: a burst/impact effect that plays at the moment a critical hit lands on an enemy, layered behind or around the floating damage number — needs to read instantly as 'extra powerful hit' during fast, continuous idle combat.

Deliverable: a style reference sheet showing the effect from several angles/stages of its burst — e.g. a grid or filmstrip-style layout with 3-4 panels showing the effect at ignition, peak burst, and fade-out — on a solid dark background (near-black, around #100d0a) so the effect's shape and colors read clearly without background noise.

Style direction: a radial burst of jagged martial-arts energy — sharp angular shockwave lines radiating outward (evoking a sword-slash or qi-burst impact rather than a soft magical sparkle), a few bright ember/spark particles flying off, hot orange-gold core color (#ff9800 core fading to #e8bc55 / #f2d27a at the edges) consistent with the game's existing critical/gold HUD accent colors, with a soft outer glow halo. The effect should feel sharp, sudden and impactful, not smooth or dreamy — like a snapping shockwave, not a magical puff of light.

Negative prompt: no soft pastel magic-sparkle look, no character or figure in the image, no text or watermark, no background scenery, no cool blue/purple color scheme (reserve those for other effect types).
```
